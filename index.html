Commercial window cleaning services and prices

A pane is one piece of glass in a frame. Prices are per side, so glass cleaned inside and outside counts twice. Prices current as of November 2026.

  • Standard window cleaning

    $2.50 per pane

    Hand-cleaned, streak-free glass. Sills and frames are wiped down with every clean at no extra charge.

  • Glass doors

    $5 per side

    Doors take more handling and more fingerprints, so each one counts as two panes.

  • Hard water and mineral restoration

    $20 per pane

    $5 per window

    Debris and grime cleared from window tracks and channels.

    $5–$15 per pane

    Common questions

    How much does commercial window cleaning cost in Exeter, NH?

    Seacoast Facility Services charges $2.50 per pane, per side, for standard cleaning and $5 per side for glass doors, with a $40 minimum per visit. A small storefront with 6 pieces of glass and 1 door, cleaned inside and outside, comes to $40. Weekly, every-2-week and monthly schedules take 20%, 15% and 10% off the standard clean.

    How is my price calculated?

    Standard cleaning is priced per pane. A pane is one piece of glass in a frame, and each side is priced separately, so a piece of glass cleaned inside and outside counts as two panes. Glass doors are priced at double a pane, per side. Hard-water or mineral staining is priced separately because it needs chemical treatment and scraping. Recurring service gets a discount off the standard clean, and every visit has a $40 minimum. The estimate tool on this page uses this exact math.

    Is the online estimate my final price?

    It's an estimate, not a final bill. If the visit turns up something the online tool couldn't see, like heavy buildup, tricky access, or extra staining, we walk you through any change before starting work.

    Why is there a first-visit charge?

    A first visit usually takes extra time to bring everything up to a clean baseline. That charge is waived automatically when you sign up for recurring service, and the estimate tool reflects that too.

    How does scheduling work?

    You're given a service week rather than an exact appointment time, and we show up in that week. Recurring accounts are rebooked automatically, so you don't have to call to get back on the schedule.

    What kinds of buildings do you clean?

    Storefronts are most of our work, but we take on most commercial window jobs. We look at every job before agreeing to it, so call or text (603) 583-9928 with the address or a photo and we'll tell you whether it's a fit.

    What areas do you serve?

    We're based in Exeter, NH and serve commercial properties throughout the Seacoast region. Not sure if you're in range? Call or text (603) 583-9928 and we'll tell you.

    Who actually shows up to do the work?

    Matt, the owner. Not a national franchise or a dispatch center. You'll have his direct number, and every estimate is free with no obligation.
